## Outcome

A user reaches the plugin tab of the right pane of the session surface with a
real pointer. The tab is inside the viewport, and a mouse click on it reaches
the tab.

## The defect

The Harness lane measured the defect in the packaged route on 2026-10-27 and
reported it in the hub messages `20261027T052637132286Z-f61e6b3e@harness` and
`20261027T084842826253Z-ff0ca50d@harness`, with the evidence in the Harness
work item `docs/workitems/doing/SESS0001.US03.SC01.three-panel-session-layout-recovery.md`:

- the failing selector is `[data-slot="session-block-right-sidebar-tab"][data-tab-key="plugins"]`;
- at 1280px the button spans x=1227 to x=1286 while the right pane spans
  x=1139 to x=1280, so 6px of the button lie outside the viewport;
- `elementFromPoint` at its centre returns the close button icon;
- the tab strip reports a scrollWidth of 129 against a clientWidth of 77;
- a real mouse click on the tab times out, while a dispatched click reaches it.

The coordinator refused the proof of UAC04 and UAC05 of the Harness scope
because of it, and returned that scope to doing; its message
`20261027T084530231511Z-b36094e0@coordinator-1` asks this lane for the exact
Components source link and checkpoint.

## The cause in this repository

`src/blocks/Session/SessionBlock.razor` renders the panels of the session
surface as three `ResizablePanel` children of one `Resizable` group. The
standalone variant gives the middle panel `DefaultSize="100"` at line 137, while
the left panel claims 16 and the right panel claims 16 at lines 11 and 366.

`src/lib/Resizable/ResizablePanel.razor` turns a size into
`flex-basis: calc({size}% - var(--resizable-handle-size, 4px))`, so the group
subscribes 16 + 100 + 16 = 132 percent of the width. Every panel then shrinks
by about a quarter, the right pane lands at 141px, and the tab strip with the
close button needs about 209px. The button of the plugin tab therefore starts
6px outside the viewport of the pane.

## The change this scope needs

The middle panel of every panel group of the session surface has to claim the
remainder of the group and not the whole of it: the size of the middle panel is
the width that the rendered side panels leave, and the groups that render one
side panel keep the sum of their panels at 100 percent.

## Acceptance

The Facts of `src/test/CBLK0002.Sessions/US01.Reachability/SC01_PluginTabReachability.cs`:

- [x] UAC01: Given a session surface with both side panes open, when the sizes of its panels are read, then they claim no more than the width of their group, so no panel is shrunk by an over-subscribed group.
- [x] UAC02: Given a session surface with both side panes open, when the sizes of its panels are read, then the panels of the group claim the whole width of the group.
- [x] UAC03: Given a session surface with a plugin surface in the right pane, when the strip of the tabs of the right pane is read, then it scrolls, so the tab of the plugin surface stays inside the pane instead of ending under the close button.
- [x] UAC04: Given the packaged session route of the Harness acceptance, when a real pointer clicks the plugin tab of the right pane, then the click reaches the tab instead of timing out.
