## Outcome

A user of the session surface sees the panels of the surface after a reset of
the profile. A persisted collapse state of a profile that no longer owns the
sessions does not keep the surface in a single panel view.

## The defect

The Harness lane reported the defect on 2026-10-27 in the hub message
`20261027T074057995925Z-c1995d9d@harness`, with the evidence in the Harness
work item `docs/workitems/doing/SESS0001.US03.SC01.three-panel-session-layout-recovery.md`:

- seeding `session-block:sidebar-open=false` and
  `session-block:right-sidebar-open=false` and reloading the packaged session
  route yields 1 panel, 0 handles, no left pane and no right pane, and a main
  pane of 1280px;
- clearing the two keys restores 3 panels, 2 handles and both side panes, and
  every linked asset answers 200.

The coordinator asked this lane to triage the reset of a stale profile into
Components source scope in its message `20261027T072921845817Z-339ca0bb@coordinator-1`.

## The owner in this repository

`src/blocks/Session/SessionBlock.razor.cs` owns the persisted state of the
panes of the surface:

- the parameter `SidebarPersistKey` defaults to `session-block:sidebar-open`
  and the parameter `RightSidebarPersistKey` defaults to
  `session-block:right-sidebar-open` at lines 76 and 77;
- the control reads both keys from the local storage at lines 433 and 503 and
  writes them back at lines 462 and 532.

The persisted state therefore belongs to the control that writes it, and the
reset of a stale profile belongs to that control as well.

## The change this scope needs

The control has to tell a collapsed pane of a live surface from a collapsed
pane of a profile that no longer owns the sessions of that surface. A persisted
state that no longer matches the state of the sessions it belongs to must not
keep the surface collapsed, and the control must be able to recover from that
state without the user clearing the storage of the browser.

## Acceptance

The Facts of `src/test/CBLK0003.Sessions/US01.StaleState/SC01_SessionSurfaceStaleState.cs`:

- [ ] UAC01: Given a surface with a live session and a persisted collapse state, when the surface renders, then the persisted state keeps the pane collapsed.
- [ ] UAC02: Given a surface whose sessions are gone and a persisted collapse state, when the surface renders, then the surface renders its panels and its handles instead of a single panel view.
- [ ] UAC03: Given a surface that recovered from a stale persisted state, when the surface renders again after a reload, then the recovery holds without the user clearing the storage of the browser.
- [ ] UAC04: Given the packaged session route of the Harness acceptance, when the route is reloaded with the stale state seeded, then it renders the three panels and the two handles and every linked asset answers 200.
